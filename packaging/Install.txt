Installing ClipDesk
===================

1. Drag ClipDesk.app onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Eject this disk image.
3. Open ClipDesk from your Applications folder. It runs in the menu bar
   (look for the clipboard icon) and opens its history window.

If macOS says ClipDesk "cannot be opened" or "cannot be verified"
-------------------------------------------------------------------
This build is not notarized by Apple (it is ad-hoc signed). To open it:

  - macOS 15 or later: try to open ClipDesk once, then go to
    System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
    "Open Anyway" next to the ClipDesk message.
  - macOS 14: Control-click ClipDesk in Applications, choose Open,
    then click Open in the dialog.

Only do this if you downloaded ClipDesk from its official GitHub
repository. You can verify the download against the published
SHA-256 checksum.

Privacy
-------
ClipDesk stores your clipboard history only on this Mac, in
~/Library/Application Support/ClipDesk. It makes no network connections.
